WorkticAI V27.15.1 — Partner Workspace UX
Build 2026-10-15

Base: V27.15 Partner Business Backoffice rebased on the current V27.13.1 production repository.
Scope: visual/layout refinement only for Partner Center. Backend, Partner accounting, CRM, billing, public website and business rules remain unchanged.

- Return to CRM shortcut.
- Tablet/mobile Partner navigation converted to an off-canvas drawer with backdrop and close control.
- Partner cards use a one-column responsive layout on tablet/mobile.
- Network tree has a larger scrollable canvas.
- Partner tables use touch-friendly horizontal responsive scrolling.
- Public V27.13.1 website and Partners landing are unchanged.

No backend/schema changes. Do NOT run /app/worktic-migrate.
